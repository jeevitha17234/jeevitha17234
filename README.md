I work at the intersection of **SAR/InSAR processing, geospatial ML, and hazard & environmental monitoring** — building pipelines that turn raw satellite/radar data into usable science.

## 🔭 Research interests
- SAR/InSAR time-series analysis — deformation, subsidence, target detection
- Radar meteorology and reflectivity reconstruction
- Applied ML/DL for remote sensing — classification, gap-filling, spatiotemporal modeling
- Geospatial hazard mapping — flood, erosion, water resources

- **Summer Intern, U R Rao Satellite Centre, ISRO** (2023) — PCA-based band selection on 15K+ Sentinel-2 scenes; ResNet50 cloud-mask classifier, 91% accuracy / 0.93 AUC
- **Summer Intern, M S Swaminathan Research Institute** (2022) — georeferencing and land-use classification for village development planning
